## Sales tips

- **Launch price:** keep the strike-through prices for the first 2 weeks, and say "launch price ends Sunday" in your Stories.
- **Reviews:** once buyers send you results, screenshot them (with permission) and add them to each product. Real reviews sell more than anything else.
- **Free guide first:** every Reel should point to the FREE Glute Starter Guide. Emails turn into sales later.
- **Order bumps:** turn them on for every product. They're the easiest extra money in your store.
- **Lead with your story:** "Single mom of 4 who rebuilt her life" makes people trust you. Mention it in your Stories often.
