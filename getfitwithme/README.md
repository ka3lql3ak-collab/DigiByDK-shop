## What's in the ZIP

The ZIP has one folder per product, each containing:
- the **PDF book** (upload to Stan as the digital download)
- the **interactive app** and the **access page** (.html)
- **Cover for Stan.png**
- **BUYER ACCESS LINK.txt**: the private link to give buyers through Stan

It also contains `STAN-STORE-COPY.md` (every title, description, button, price and order bump) and all the cover images.

## How buyers get their product

Buyer pays on Stan → Stan gives them their **access link** → a thank-you page with a big **Open your interactive program** button and a **Download your PDF book** button. The first page of every PDF also has a **Tap to open the interactive program** button.

## Editing

Change `_build/content.py`, `_build/library.py` or `_build/guides/*.md`, then run `python3 getfitwithme/_build/build.py --pdf`.
