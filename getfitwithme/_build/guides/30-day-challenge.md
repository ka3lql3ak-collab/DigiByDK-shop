## Welcome: How the Challenge Works

For the next 30 days, you'll complete a short daily workout, four simple daily habits and a mindset prompt. The goal isn't perfection. It's **consistency**. Thirty days of showing up rewires your habits, your energy and how you see yourself.

### Your daily routine

1. **Workout (20–35 min):** circuits you can do at home or at the gym. Dumbbells optional.
2. **4 non-negotiables:** workout, 80+ oz of water, 8,000 steps, protein at every meal.
3. **Mindset prompt:** 2–5 minutes of journaling.

## How to Do the Circuits

Each workout is a **circuit**: do every exercise once in order with minimal rest, then rest 60 seconds and repeat for the listed number of rounds.

### Timing tips

- **Reps** (e.g. "15") means complete that many controlled reps.
- **Seconds** (e.g. "30s") means work continuously for that time. Use the timer.
- **/side** means do the reps on each side.

### Intensity

The last few reps of each exercise should feel challenging. If a round feels easy, slow down the lowering phase, add a pause or hold dumbbells.

::quiz
Q: What does "10 /side" mean?

* 10 reps on each side

E: Do 10 reps on the left and 10 on the right.

## Scaling Every Move

Every exercise can be made easier or harder. Pick the version that challenges you while keeping good form.

| Exercise | Easier | Harder |
|---|---|---|
| Push-ups | Hands on a bench or wall | Feet elevated, or slow 3-sec lowering |
| Squats | Squat to a chair | Hold a dumbbell (goblet), or pause at the bottom |
| Glute bridges | Shorter range | Single-leg or dumbbell on hips |
| Reverse lunges | Hold a wall for balance | Hold dumbbells, or step back onto a deficit |
| Burpees | Step back instead of jumping, no push-up | Add a tuck jump |
| Mountain climbers | Slow, hands on a bench | Faster pace |
| Squat jumps | Bodyweight squats with a calf raise | Deeper, higher jumps |
| Plank | On knees | Add shoulder taps |
| Tricep dips | Bend knees more | Feet on a chair |
| Donkey kicks / fire hydrants | Smaller range | Add a mini band |
| Leg raises | Bent knees | Slower, legs lower |

> **Joint-friendly swaps:** If jumping bothers your knees, swap squat jumps for fast bodyweight squats and burpees for squat-to-stand reaches. You'll get the same results.

## Why These 4 Habits?

### 1. Daily workout

Regular movement improves strength, mood, sleep and energy. A short daily habit beats an occasional long one.

### 2. 80+ oz of water

Good hydration improves energy, training performance and appetite control. Many people mistake thirst for hunger. Adjust upward if you're bigger, sweat a lot or live somewhere hot.

### 3. 8,000 steps

Daily movement (NEAT) can burn hundreds of calories more than a workout. Research links higher daily step counts to better heart health and lower mortality, with benefits continuing up to roughly 8,000–10,000 steps for most adults.

**How to get your steps:** a 10-minute walk after each meal, phone calls while walking, parking further away, and walking meetings.

### 4. Protein at every meal

Protein keeps you full, protects muscle and helps you recover from workouts. Aim for a palm-sized portion (or more) at every meal. See the protein table below.

| Easy protein source | Approx. protein |
|---|---|
| 3 eggs | 18 g |
| 1 cup Greek yogurt | 20–23 g |
| 4 oz chicken breast | 35 g |
| 1 can tuna | 25 g |
| 1 scoop protein powder | 20–25 g |
| 1 cup cottage cheese | 26 g |

## Mindset & Consistency

### Identity beats motivation

Motivation comes and goes. Instead of "I'm trying to work out", think "**I'm someone who moves every day**". Each completed day is a vote for that identity.

### Never miss twice

You will have a bad day. That's normal. The rule: **never miss two days in a row**. One missed day is an accident. Two is the start of a new habit.

### Track your wins

Check off every habit in the tracker. Watching your streak grow makes you want to protect it.

### Use the journal prompts

Writing turns vague feelings into clear insight. Spend at least 2 minutes on each prompt, especially on hard days.

::quiz

E: One missed day is an accident. Getting straight back on track is what keeps the habit alive.
::

| 2 | Motivation dips. This is the most important week. Lean on your "why" from Day 1 |
| 3 | Workouts feel easier even though they're harder. Energy and sleep improve |

## Rest, Recovery & Soreness

- **Active Recovery days:** keep moving gently. Walking and stretching speed recovery.
- **Rest & Reset days:** real rest, plus meal prep and your weekly photo.
- **Soreness:** normal in Week 1. Gentle movement helps. Sharp or joint pain means stop and modify.
- **Sleep:** aim for 7–9 hours. It's when your body recovers and adapts.
