## Glute Basics in 2 Minutes

Your glutes are three muscles:

- **Gluteus maximus:** the big one that creates size and shape. Trained by hip extension: hip thrusts, bridges, RDLs, lunges.
- **Gluteus medius & minimus:** on the side of your hip. Trained by abduction: banded walks, abduction machine, fire hydrants.

Training all three from different angles is how you build round, full glutes.

## The 4 Rules for Glute Growth

### 1. Progressive overload

Each week, add a rep or a little weight. If you do the same thing every week, your body has no reason to change.

### 2. Train close to failure

Stop each set when you could only do about **1–3 more good reps**. Easy sets don't build muscle.

### 3. Control every rep

Lower slowly for about 2 seconds, squeeze at the top. Momentum steals tension from your glutes.

### 4. Eat enough protein

Aim for **0.7–1.0 g of protein per pound of goal body weight** daily. Muscle is built from protein.

## Technique Essentials

**Hip thrusts & bridges:** chin tucked, ribs down, drive through your heels and squeeze at the top. If you feel it in your lower back, you're arching. Tilt your pelvis under instead.

**RDLs:** push your hips back, keep the weights close to your legs and stop when your hamstrings are fully stretched. Your back stays flat.

**Split squats & lunges:** lean your torso slightly forward and push through your front heel for more glute focus.
